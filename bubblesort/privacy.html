Privacy Policy

Bubbly Bakes · Crazy Games Developer · Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: this game collects nothing. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no purchases, and it does not send anything about you anywhere. Everything below is the detail behind that sentence.

1. We do not ask who you are

The game has no account, no sign-in and no profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There is nothing to log into and nothing to fill in.

2. Your progress stays on your device

Your current level, your coins and boosters, and your settings (sound, music, vibration and language) are written to local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded, we cannot read them, and we hold no copy. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

3. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising

This version contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no advertising SDK. No advertising identifier is read. The game makes no network requests of its own, so no device identifier, IP address or usage event reaches us or any third party through it.

We have nothing to sell, rent or share, because we hold nothing.

4. Rating the game on Google Play

After you have played for a while, the game may ask Google Play to show its standard "rate this app" card. That card is displayed and handled entirely by Google Play: we do not see whether it was shown, whether you rated, or what you wrote. A rating or review you choose to post is public on Google Play and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

When the game starts, it asks Google Play whether a newer version of Bubbly Bakes is available, and Google Play may download and install it for you. Google Play answers this from the information it already has about your device and the installed version; the game sends nothing of its own, and we do not receive any of it. This is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. The one case where another company sees a request

If you tap the Privacy Policy link inside the game, your device opens this website in your browser. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive that request, including your IP address, the same way any website visit works. That is a visit to a web page, not data the game sends. GitHub's handling of it is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. No in-app purchases

There are no purchases, subscriptions or paid currency in this version. Coins and boosters are earned by playing and cannot be bought, so no payment data exists and none is processed.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. Because the game collects no data at all, no data from a child is collected either.

8. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), the LGPD (Brazil) and the CCPA (California) give you the right to see, correct or delete data a company holds about you. In our case the honest answer is that there is nothing to give you, correct or delete, because we hold no data about you. The only data that exists is your save file, on your own device:

  • To delete it: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • To ask us anything about this: write to the address in section 11.
  • You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not charge for any request and we do not treat you differently for making one.

9. Security

The strongest protection for personal data is not to collect it, which is the approach this game takes.

10. Changes

If a future update adds analytics, crash reporting, advertising or purchases, this page will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected and by whom, the date at the top will change, and the Google Play "Data safety" section for the app will be updated to match. The policy changes before the release does.
