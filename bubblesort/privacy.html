Privacy Policy

Bubbly Bakes · Crazy Games Developer · Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: no account, no advertising, no purchases, no advertising ID. If you agree on the privacy screen when the game first starts, it sends crash reports and play statistics to Google Firebase so we can fix crashes and tune the levels. If you choose "No thanks", it sends nothing. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy Policy. Everything below is the detail behind that.

1. We do not ask who you are

The game has no account, no sign-in and no profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There is nothing to log into and nothing to fill in.

2. Your progress stays on your device

Your current level, your coins and boosters, and your settings (sound, music, vibration and language) are written to local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded, we cannot read them, and we hold no copy. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

3. Crash reports and play statistics (only if you agree)

When the game first starts it shows a privacy screen. Nothing below is sent before you answer it, and nothing at all if you choose "No thanks". If you choose "Accept", the game uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): when the game crashes or hits an error, a report with the error and its stack trace, the device model, the Android version, free memory and disk, screen orientation, the game version, and a random installation identifier.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (play statistics): which levels you start and finish (level number, difficulty, attempt number, win or loss, time spent, revives used), restarting a level, using a Hint or a Shuffle, unlocking a shelf and reviving (and whether coins were used), and the highest level you have reached; plus what Firebase records by itself: the first launch, sessions and their length, app updates, the device model, Android version, language, country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored), the game version and a random app instance identifier.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID (the advertising ID permission is removed from the game, and advertising features are switched off). We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with anyone: Google processes it for us as our service provider, under the Firebase data processing terms and the Google Privacy Policy. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates.

How long: play statistics are kept for 14 months, crash reports for 90 days, then deleted.

Changing your mind: Settings → Privacy Policy → "Stop sharing" (or "Share data"). Stopping turns statistics off at once and crash reports off from the next start of the game.

The game also asks Google Firebase Remote Config, after you have agreed, for two settings that control update prompts. That request carries the random installation identifier and the game version, nothing else.

4. Rating the game on Google Play

After you have played for a while, the game may ask Google Play to show its standard "rate this app" card. That card is displayed and handled entirely by Google Play: we do not see whether it was shown, whether you rated, or what you wrote. A rating or review you choose to post is public on Google Play and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

When the game starts, it asks Google Play whether a newer version of Bubbly Bakes is available, and Google Play may download and install it for you. Google Play answers this from the information it already has about your device and the installed version; the game sends nothing of its own, and we do not receive any of it. This is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. The one case where another company sees a request

If you tap the Privacy Policy link inside the game, your device opens this website in your browser. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive that request, including your IP address, the same way any website visit works. That is a visit to a web page, not data the game sends. GitHub's handling of it is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. No in-app purchases

There are no purchases, subscriptions or paid currency in this version. Coins and boosters are earned by playing and cannot be bought, so no payment data exists and none is processed.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has sent us crash reports or statistics, write to us and we will delete them.

8. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), the LGPD (Brazil) and the CCPA (California) give you the right to see, correct or delete data a company holds about you. We hold no name or contact data; the statistics and crash reports are tied only to the random identifiers above:

  • To stop sharing: Settings → Privacy Policy → "Stop sharing".
  • To delete what was sent: write to the address in section 11. We delete the statistics and crash reports of your installation with Firebase's deletion tools; if we cannot find them from what you send us, we tell you.
  • To delete your progress: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not charge for any request and we do not treat you differently for making one.

9. Security

We collect as little as the two purposes above need, nothing before your answer, and everything is sent encrypted. Access to the Firebase project is limited to the developer's accounts.

10. Changes

If a future update changes what is collected, or adds advertising or purchases, this page will be updated first to describe exactly what is collected and by whom, the date at the top will change, the Google Play "Data safety" section will be updated to match, and the game will show its privacy screen again. The policy changes before the release does.
