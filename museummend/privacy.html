Privacy Policy

Museum Mend: Art Jigsaw Puzzle · Crazy Games Developer · Version 1 · Effective: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Museum Mend: Art Jigsaw Puzzle (the "Game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The Game is published on Google Play by Crazy Games Developer ("we", "us"). Questions: contact@crazygamesdeveloper.com.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not run our own servers. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The Game shows ads. Our advertising partners collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described in section 4. You can limit this in the Game's Privacy options and in your Android settings.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, so we can fix the Game (section 7).
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played (for example which levels are hard), only after the consent choice in the Game where the law requires it (section 7). It does not use your advertising ID.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (restored paintings, chapter, settings such as sound and music, and whether you bought Remove Ads) in the Game's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup, under Google's terms. Uninstalling the Game deletes the local data.

3. Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not operate servers for the Game and do not receive personal information from it. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. We delete support emails within 12 months unless a longer period is required by law.

4. Advertising

The Game is free to play and shows ads: full-screen ads between levels, a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, an ad in a box on some level result screens and, when you choose to watch one for a reward, rewarded ads. New players see none of these until they have finished the first levels. We use Google AdMob to decide which advertising network serves each ad. When an ad is requested, shown or tapped, the advertising software in the Game and the ad networks listed below may collect:

  • Device or other identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and similar identifiers;
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (never GPS);
  • App interactions: ad views, taps, app launches and time in the Game;
  • Diagnostics: device model, operating system, language, screen size, app version, launch time and performance data.

They use this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where the law allows and you agree, show ads based on your interests. This information is shared with the ad networks below and is encrypted in transit.

Your advertising choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where it is required): before any ad loads, the Game shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the Game.
  • US state privacy laws: where required, the same Privacy options screen lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device). Without it you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Remove Ads: the one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads shown between levels, the banner ad and the ads on result screens. Rewarded ads, if offered, only play when you tap to watch one.

We ask our partners to show only ads suitable for a general audience (ad content rating PG).

5. In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The Game talks to Google Play through the Unity In-App Purchasing library, on your device only (we do not use Unity Gaming Services, and the Game sends no purchase data to Unity). It only receives a confirmation of the purchase from Google Play and remembers on your device that ads are removed. We do not receive your name, email, card or other payment details.

6. App updates and ratings

The Game uses Google Play's in-app update and in-app review features. When the Game checks for an update or offers a rating prompt, Google Play processes information such as your device model, Android version and the Game's version to answer that request. This happens between your device and Google Play, under Google's terms; we do not receive this information.

7. Crash reports, statistics and game settings

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix bugs. When the Game crashes, or when an error occurs in the Game's code, Crashlytics sends a report with crash logs (the technical error and where in the code it happened) and diagnostics (device model and brand, Android version, app version, free memory and disk, whether the Game was in the foreground, the time of the crash), together with a random Crashlytics installation identifier and a Firebase installation identifier that are not linked to your advertising ID, your name or your Google account. Error messages are filtered on your device to remove e-mail addresses, web addresses and long numbers before they are sent. This data is processed by Google as our service provider, is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared with other companies, and is encrypted in transit. Crash reports are kept for 90 days and then deleted. More: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The Game also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive updated game settings (such as how often ads between levels may appear or how many hints a rewarded video gives). To do this it sends the Firebase installation identifier and the app version; it receives only settings, which take effect the next time you open the Game.

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played, so we can tune difficulty, hints and ad frequency. It collects app interactions (levels started, completed or left, with the grid size, number of moves, time and hints used; tutorial steps; hints used; ads offered, shown or failed, why an ad was or was not shown (for example no ad loaded or no connection) and rewards earned; the Remove Ads screen opened), purchase history (Google Play reports in-app purchases such as Remove Ads, with product, price and currency, to Google Analytics; we never receive payment details), diagnostics (device model, Android version, app version, language, session start and length) and two random identifiers created by Firebase for this installation (the app instance identifier and the Firebase installation identifier). It does not collect your advertising ID, your name, your email or your precise location, and we do not link it with advertising. Collection starts switched off: it is switched on only after the consent message has been answered and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, only if you agreed to the storage of information on your device and to measurement; if you did not, nothing is sent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Google processes this data as our service provider, encrypted in transit, and does not sell it or share it with other companies. Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.
  • We keep no data from the Game on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies (linked above); you can ask them directly, and resetting or deleting your advertising ID stops new data from being linked to the old identifier.
  • Crash reports (section 7) are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can ask us to delete them sooner: email us the approximate date and your device model, and we delete the matching reports within 30 days.
  • Analytics data (section 7) is deleted automatically after 2 months. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage, ends the link to the old identifiers; you can also ask us to delete the analytics data of your installation and we answer within 30 days.
  • If you need help with a request about your data, email contact@crazygamesdeveloper.com. We answer within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the UK, Brazil or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Withdraw consent for ads in Settings → Privacy options; for anything else, contact us. Legal bases (EEA/UK): your consent for personalised advertising, and our legitimate interest in keeping the Game free, secure and working for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and bug fixing. In the EEA/UK, game statistics (section 7) are collected only with your consent.

11. Security and international transfers

Our partners encrypt data in transit (TLS). They may process it in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies.
