Privacy Policy

Lumen Sandbox · Crazy Games Developer · Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: Lumen Sandbox is an internal test app, installed only by people we invite through Google Play's internal testing. It shows ads and measures how the app is used, through the third-party services listed below. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's settings.

1. We do not ask who you are

The app has no account, no sign-in and no profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos or contacts.

2. Your progress stays on your device

Test progress and settings are written to local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through AppLovin MAX, which selects ads from AppLovin and from other ad networks, including Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads, these companies collect from your device:

  • the device's advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • how you interact with ads, for example whether an ad was shown, watched to the end or tapped.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent (section 5). If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised.

The companies' own policies: AppLovin Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Analytics and remote settings

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count events such as starting a level or watching a rewarded ad, and Firebase Remote Config to change test settings, such as how often ads appear, without an update. Firebase collects an app instance identifier, device information, an approximate location derived from the IP address, and the events the app records. Where consent is required, analytics storage follows your consent choice. See the Firebase privacy information.

5. Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form provided by Google (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) before any ad is requested. You can accept, decline or choose per purpose. You can reopen the form at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app. Declining does not stop the app from working.

6. Test purchases

The app may offer in-app purchases for testing. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google Payments' privacy notice. We receive only the order identifier and which product was bought, never your payment details.

7. The case where another company sees a request

If you tap the Privacy Policy link inside the app, your device opens this website in your browser. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive that request, including your IP address, the same way any website visit works. GitHub's handling of it is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), the LGPD (Brazil) and the CCPA (California) give you the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for its use.

  • To change your ad and analytics consent: use Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • To limit ad tracking: reset or delete your advertising identifier in your Android settings.
  • To delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • For data held by AppLovin, Google or Firebase: they act on such requests under the policies linked above; write to the address in section 11 and we will help you reach them.
  • You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal data. We do not charge for any request and we do not treat you differently for making one.

10. Changes

If the services this app uses change, this page is updated first, and the date at the top changes.
