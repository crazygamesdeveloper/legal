Privacy Policy

Jewelry Untangle · Crazy Games Developer · Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: the game has no account, no advertising and no purchases. It does send crash reports and basic gameplay events so that we can fix crashes and see which levels are too hard. None of it identifies you.

1. We do not ask who you are

The game has no account, no sign-in and no profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There is nothing to log into and nothing to fill in.

2. Your progress stays on your device

Which levels you have finished, your stars and your settings are written to local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded, we cannot read them, and we hold no copy. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

3. Diagnostics and gameplay data

To find crashes and to see which levels are too hard, the game uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics). Nothing is collected before you accept this policy on the first screen of the game. After that, these are collected:

WhatWhy
Crash reports: stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory at the timeto fix the crash
Gameplay events: app opened, session length, level started, finished, restarted or left unfinished, the level number, how long it took, how many pieces were freed and boosters used, the highest level reachedto see where players get stuck
Approximate region at country level, derived from IP address; we do not store the IP addressto read the numbers per market
A pseudonymous app instance identifier generated by Firebaseto count one device as one player

This data is not linked to your identity, and we do not try to re-identify you from it. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and no advertising identifier is read.

4. No advertising, no purchases, no selling of data

This version contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases. We do not sell, rent or trade data about you, and we do not share it with data brokers.

5. Who else processes the data

Only Google, as our processor for Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics; their handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Separately, if you tap the Terms or Privacy link in the game, your browser visits this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive that request including your IP address — that is an ordinary web page visit, not data the game sends. We may also disclose data if we are legally required to, which has not happened.

When a newer version of the game is on Google Play, the game asks the Google Play app on your device to show its update screen (Google Play In-App Updates), and you need the newer version to keep playing. This check goes through the Google Play app already on your device; the game itself sends no data for it.

6. How long it is kept

Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Analytics events are retained for up to 14 months. Local save data stays on your device until you delete the game.

7. No in-app purchases

There are no purchases, subscriptions or paid currency in this version, so no payment data exists and none is processed.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and you want the associated records removed, contact us and we will delete them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA in California) you may ask us to confirm what data relates to you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop processing it. Because the game holds no identifier that points to you as a person, the practical steps are:

  • Delete local data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Delete diagnostics data: write to us. Tell us roughly when you played and on which device model, and we will delete the matching records we can identify.
  • Complain: you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not charge for any request and we do not treat you differently for making one.

10. Security

Data in transit to Firebase is encrypted with TLS. No system is perfect, so we keep what we collect to the minimum listed in section 3 — the best protection for data is not to hold it.

11. Changes

If we add anything that collects more data — advertising, purchases, or leaderboards showing player names — we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released, and update the Google Play "Data safety" section to match.
