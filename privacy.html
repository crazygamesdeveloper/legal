Privacy Policy

Jewelry Untangle · Crazy Games Developer · Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: the game has no account. It shows ads through Google AdMob, it sells a few optional items through Google Play (Remove Ads and ring skins), and it sends crash reports and basic gameplay events so that we can fix crashes and see which levels are too hard. Payment is handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it asks for your consent before ads use your data, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's settings.

1. We do not ask who you are

The game has no account, no sign-in and no profile. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There is nothing to log into and nothing to fill in.

2. Your progress stays on your device

Which levels you have finished, your stars, the hearts you have collected, the ring skins you own or unlocked with hearts, and your settings are written to local storage on your own device. They are not uploaded, we cannot read them, and we hold no copy. Uninstalling the game deletes them (items bought through Google Play come back on a new install: see section 5).

3. Advertising

The game shows full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. The ads come from Google AdMob, which selects them from Google and from the ad technology partners named in the consent form (section 4). No ad is requested before you accept this policy on the first screen of the game. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google and those partners collect from your device:

  • the device's advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings) and an app set identifier;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • how you interact with ads, for example whether an ad was shown, watched to the end or tapped.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and settings (sections 4 and 11). If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form provided by Google (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) before any ad uses your data. You can consent, decline, or choose per purpose and per partner. Your choice is stored on your device. You can reopen the form at any time from Settings → About Us → Privacy Settings in the game. Declining does not stop the game from working.

5. In-app purchases

The game sells a few optional items, each bought once and kept: Remove Ads, which stops the full-screen ads between levels (the rewarded ads you choose to watch stay available), the ring skins and an All Skins pack. Ring skins can also be unlocked with the hearts you collect in the game, which involves no payment and no data leaving your device. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under its own terms; we never see your name, card or payment details. Google Play tells the game which items you own, and the game keeps that on your device so the items work offline. The game does not contact Google Play for purchases before you accept this policy on the first screen of the game.

6. Diagnostics and gameplay data

To find crashes and to see which levels are too hard, the game uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics), and Firebase Remote Config to receive settings such as how often ads appear. Nothing is collected before you accept this policy on the first screen of the game. After that, these are collected:

WhatWhy
Crash reports: stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory at the timeto fix the crash
Gameplay events: app opened, session length, level started, finished, restarted or left unfinished, the level number, how long it took, how many pieces were freed and boosters used, and the highest level reachedto see where players get stuck
Approximate region at country level, derived from IP address; we do not store the IP addressto read the numbers per market
A pseudonymous app instance identifier generated by Firebaseto count one device as one player
Purchases: which item was bought, cancelled or failed, and its listed price and currency (no order number, account or payment details); Firebase also records Google Play purchases by itselfto count revenue and see whether the shop works
A settings request to Firebase Remote Config: the app version, the device's language and country, and a Firebase installation identifierto switch ads off or change how often they appear without an update

This data is not linked to your identity, and we do not try to re-identify you from it. Firebase does not receive the advertising identifier, and we do not use this data for advertising or profiling. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics follows your choice in the consent form.

7. No selling of data

We do not sell data about you for money, and we do not share it with data brokers. The advertising data in section 3 goes to Google and its ad partners only so that they can show and measure ads in the game.

8. Who else processes the data

Google, for AdMob (section 3), for Google Play, which processes the payments for in-app purchases (section 5), and as our processor for Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics and Remote Config (section 6); their handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and the ad partners in the consent form follow their own policies. Separately, if you tap the Terms or Privacy link in the game, your browser visits this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers receive that request including your IP address — that is an ordinary web page visit, not data the game sends. We may also disclose data if we are legally required to, which has not happened.

When a newer version of the game is on Google Play, the game asks the Google Play app on your device to show its update screen (Google Play In-App Updates), and you need the newer version to keep playing. This check goes through the Google Play app already on your device; the game itself sends no data for it.

The game may also ask Google Play to show its rating card (Google Play In-App Review). That card is shown by the Google Play app on your device; the game does not see whether or how you rate it.

If you allow notifications, the game reminds you to come back one day and three days after you last played. These reminders are scheduled on your device itself and taken back when you return; no data leaves your device for them. You can turn them off at any time in your Android settings for the app.

9. How long it is kept

Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Analytics events are retained for up to 14 months. Advertising data is kept by Google and its partners under their own policies. Your order history stays in your Google account under Google Play's terms. Your consent choice, the record of the items you own and local save data stay on your device until you delete the game.

10. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and you want the associated records removed, contact us and we will delete them.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA in California) you may ask us to confirm what data relates to you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop processing it. Because the game holds no identifier that points to you as a person, the practical steps are:

  • Change your ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Settings → About Us → Privacy Settings in the game.
  • Stop personalised ads anywhere: delete or reset your advertising identifier in your Android settings (on most devices Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Under some US state laws, using the advertising identifier for personalised ads can count as "sharing"; deleting it opts you out.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Delete diagnostics data: write to us. Tell us roughly when you played and on which device model, and we will delete the matching records we can identify.
  • Data held by Google for ads: Google acts on such requests under the policies linked in section 3; write to us and we will help you reach them.
  • Purchases and refunds: your order history and refunds are handled by Google Play (Google Play refund help).
  • Complain: you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not charge for any request and we do not treat you differently for making one.

12. Security

Data in transit to Firebase, to Google Play and to Google's ad servers is encrypted with TLS. No system is perfect, so we keep what we collect to the minimum listed in sections 3, 5 and 6 — the best protection for data is not to hold it.

13. Changes

If we add anything that collects more data — another ad network, or leaderboards showing player names — we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released, and update the Google Play "Data safety" section to match.
